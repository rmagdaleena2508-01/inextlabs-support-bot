## Products

### inFlow EngageAI
AI agents for customer conversations, automation, and engagement. An agentic conversational AI solution that powers customer support, IT automation, sales, and lead generation. Enables faster responses, smarter workflows, and reduced operational costs.

Sub-products:
- EngageAI for Sales Intelligence
- EngageAI for Customer Service

### inFlow DocsAI
Intelligent document processing and data extraction. An agentic document intelligence solution that reads, validates, and acts on documents — automating operations, ensuring compliance, and turning documents into decisions.

### inFlow InsightsAI
Conversational analytics and enterprise data insights. An agentic analytics solution that lets teams ask questions in natural language and get instant, reasoned insights — explaining data, uncovering opportunities, and guiding better decisions.

## How the Three Products Work Together
The three agents (EngageAI, DocsAI, InsightsAI) are not separate tools but a single interconnected intelligence:
- When EngageAI captures a customer conversation, DocsAI instantly processes any referenced documents, while InsightsAI surfaces the data patterns behind the request.
- Each agent makes the others smarter. Insights from analytics refine engagement strategies. Document intelligence enriches conversation context.
- Together they transform how organizations think, decide, and act across every department and workflow.
